# AMAZON_INDIA_SALES_DASHBOARD

An interactive Amazon India Sales Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, order trends, product performance, customer order outcomes, payment methods, fulfillment models, and geographic sales distribution.The dashboard combines data analysis, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, and interactive slicers

Executive Summary

The Amazon India dataset generated ₹15.58 Crore in sales and ₹3.32 Crore in profit across 10,000 orders and 24,926 units sold. Electronics & Mobiles is the dominant category, contributing approximately 79.3% of total sales. UPI is the leading payment method with around 50.4% of sales, while Amazon FBA handles approximately 69.0% of sales. Maharashtra is the highest-sales state. The overall return and cancellation rates are 4.85% and 5.00%, respectively, with Electronics & Mobiles showing the highest return rate and Apparel & Fashion showing the highest cancellation rate.
